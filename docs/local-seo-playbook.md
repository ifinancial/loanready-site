## 1. Do these first (week 1)

These steps decide whether Google can rank the site at all.

1. **Connect the custom domain.** Every page tells Google its home is `loanready.goifinancial.com`. Until that address works, rankings won't build. In Netlify: Domain management → add `loanready.goifinancial.com`. In Hostinger DNS: CNAME `loanready` → `loanready-ifinancial.netlify.app`.
2. **Google Search Console** (free): add the property, verify through Hostinger DNS, submit `https://loanready.goifinancial.com/sitemap.xml`, then use "URL inspection → Request indexing" on the homepage, the 3 service pages, the MCA page and the 8 guides.
3. **Bing Webmaster Tools** (free): sign in and choose "Import from Google Search Console." It takes 2 minutes. Bing also powers ChatGPT search and Copilot results.
4. **Google Business Profile.** Claim or update it (details in section 4). For local searches this matters more than anything else.
5. **Fix the old Finance Solutions Group listing.** If it's still on Google with an old address or phone, update it to match the new information, or request that it be marked "moved." Two listings with different information split your reviews and confuse Google.
6. **One business name, address and phone (NAP), everywhere, written exactly the same way:**

```
iFinancial
751 Northlake Blvd, Suite 2D
North Palm Beach, FL 33408
(772) 262-5435
```

Use "Loan Ready by iFinancial" as the program name on the website and in posts. On listings, use the name that's on your door and signage. Google requires the real-world business name. Don't add keywords like "iFinancial Credit Repair North Palm Beach" to the name; Google suspends profiles for that.

## 5. Reviews: the biggest ranking lever you control

- **Get your review link:** in your profile, go to "Ask for reviews" and copy the short link.
- **Text it to every client** at a happy moment: a deletion, a funding, a paid-off MCA. Use: *"Thank you for trusting us! Would you mind sharing your experience? It helps other business owners find us: [link]"*
- **Goal:** 4–6 new reviews a month, steadily. A burst followed by nothing looks unnatural.
- **Reply to every review** within 48 hours, good or bad. Never confirm someone is a credit repair client in a reply; keep it general.
- **Never offer discounts or gifts for reviews,** and never write or buy them. That violates Google's policies and the FTC's 2024 rule on fake and incentivized reviews.

## 9. Realistic expectations

- **Google Business Profile:** map rankings near the office can start moving within weeks once the profile is complete and reviews are coming in.
- **Organic search:** new sites typically take 3–6 months to rank for competitive local terms. Lower-competition guides (like "SBA can't pay off MCA") can show up sooner.
- **No one can guarantee rankings.** Anyone who does is selling something. Consistency wins: weekly Google posts, steady reviews, 2 guides a month.

## 10. Compliance notes for marketing

- No guarantees of score increases, deletions or approvals in any ad, post or video.
- Credit repair fees are only charged after the work is performed. Keep that language consistent everywhere.
- Credit repair sold over the phone falls under the FTC's Telemarketing Sales Rule. Close credit repair in person or by video, and have your attorney review your scripts.
- Use client names and results only with written permission.
