Why underwriters care about your books

The fastest way to get turned down is a P&L that doesn't match your tax return, or a tax return that shows almost no profit. Lenders size your loan off documented income. If the books are behind or every possible expense is written off, the bank sees a business that can't afford the payment.

Save on taxes without killing your borrowing power

Most owners are told to write off everything. That saves money in April, and then the bank sees a business that barely breaks even. Our in-house accountants and CPA plan your taxes with your next loan in mind. You legally keep more from Uncle Sam, and your returns still show the income an underwriter needs.

The timing matters too. We plan when to file, when to take distributions, and when to apply, so the documents a lender pulls tell the strongest true story about your business.

What's included

  • Monthly bookkeeping. Categorized transactions, bank and credit card reconciliations, and a monthly close.
  • Lender-ready statements. A P&L and balance sheet every month, in the format underwriters expect.
  • Year-round tax planning. Our CPA and accountants work with you during the year, not just at filing time. Together you balance lower taxes against showing the income your next loan needs.
  • Debt service tracking. We track how much new debt your cash flow can support. You'll know what you can borrow before you apply.
  • Loan file package. Statements, returns, aging reports, and a personal financial statement, organized for the lender.
