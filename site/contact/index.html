Who we are

Part of iFinancial. Built to close the gap.

iFinancial bridges the gap between small business owners and the bankers who lend to them. We arrange everything from same-day funding to SBA, asset-based and long-term loans.

Loan Ready is the other half of that work. It's for the owners and families who aren't approvable yet. Our in-house credit team, bookkeeping and tax planning get the file ready, and then iFinancial gets it funded.
