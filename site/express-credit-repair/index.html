Who Express is for

You have a deal in front of you: a building, a piece of equipment, an SBA loan, or a home. The lender came back with a short list of items to clean up. Express is a concentrated round focused on exactly those items. Your file goes to the front of the line.

How it works

  • Day 1: Review in the office or by video. We pull your three reports and go over the lender's conditions with you. Then we agree on which items to target.
  • Days 2–30: Priority round. Our team prepares and sends disputes on the targeted items and tracks every response.
  • End of round: Report and billing. You get an updated three-bureau report. The $1,500 program fee is billed after the round. The $300 per-item fee applies only to items that were deleted.
  • Next: Back to the lender. If you're ready, iFinancial takes the updated file to the lender, or to a better one.

An honest note on timing

Bureaus generally have 30 days to investigate a dispute. Many respond sooner, but some take the full window. We can't control their timeline or guarantee any specific deletion. We can make sure your round is prepared well and goes out fast.

Express or monthly?

If you have a closing date or a lender waiting, choose Express. If you're six months or more from applying, the $250/month program is usually the better value. You can start with Express and move to monthly afterward.

Common questions

What counts as a deleted item?

An item that is removed from one of your credit reports as a result of our dispute, confirmed on your updated report. If an item is deleted from all three bureaus, we'll explain upfront how that is counted in your agreement.

What if nothing is deleted?

Then no per-item fees apply. We'll review the results with you and lay out the next step honestly.

Can Express help with a mortgage?

Yes. Mortgage lenders use your middle score, so moving one bureau can matter. We'll target the bureau that will move the middle score.
