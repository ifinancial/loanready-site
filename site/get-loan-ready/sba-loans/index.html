7(a), 504 and SBA Express

Get ready for SBA Loans.

SBA loans have the longest terms and some of the lowest rates small businesses can get. They also have the most paperwork, and they're where a lot of owners get turned down.

What lenders look at

  • Personal credit for every owner with 20% or more of the business
  • Two to three years of business and personal tax returns
  • Cash flow that covers the new payment with room to spare (debt service coverage)
  • Year-to-date P&L and balance sheet
  • Any past government debt, defaults, or open tax liens
  • A clear use of funds

Where applicants get stuck

  • Tax returns that show too little income because every expense was written off
  • Late payments or collections on one owner's personal report
  • Books that don't match the tax returns
  • Missing interim financials

How Loan Ready fixes it

We clean up the personal credit side, rebuild the books so they tie to your returns, and our in-house CPA and tax team make sure next year's return shows the income a lender needs to see. When the file is ready, iFinancial takes it to SBA lenders who match your profile.
