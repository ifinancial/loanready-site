You can do this yourself

You have the right to dispute inaccurate information in your credit report by contacting the credit bureau directly, at no cost. Neither you nor any credit repair company has the right to have accurate, current and verifiable information removed from your credit report.

What we do and don't promise

  • We do not guarantee any specific score increase, deletion, or result.
  • We do not guarantee loan approval. Lenders make all credit decisions.
  • We never advise you to create a new credit identity or give false information to anyone.

When you pay

We do not charge for credit repair services before they are performed. Monthly program fees are billed after each month's services are completed. Express Repair fees are billed after the round is completed, and per-item fees apply only to items deleted.

Your right to cancel

You can cancel your contract without penalty within the time period stated in your agreement, as required by the federal Credit Repair Organizations Act and Florida law. Your agreement explains exactly how to cancel.

Free credit reports

You can get free credit reports from all three bureaus at AnnualCreditReport.com.

Questions or complaints

Contact us at info@goifinancial.com or 772-262-5435. You can also contact the Consumer Financial Protection Bureau, the Federal Trade Commission, or the Florida Attorney General.
